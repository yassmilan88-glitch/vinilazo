    Every disc has a story

    These aren't random goals: we picked every one. Here's why they made history.

    1Hurst Wembley 1966

    1966 World Cup final at Wembley against West Germany. Geoff Hurst scores a hat-trick, the last in the final seconds of extra time. 4-2.

    Did you know? His second, the one that bounced down off the bar, is still argued about. It's England's only World Cup.

    «They think it's all over… it is now!»

    — Kenneth Wolstenholme, BBC

    2Platt vs Belgium 1990

    Italia 90 last 16 in Bologna against Belgium, 119th minute. From Gascoigne's free kick, David Platt swivels and volleys in: 1-0.

    Did you know? He had come off the bench. The goal opened the door to Italian football for him.

    3Gascoigne vs Scotland 1996

    Euro 96 at Wembley against Scotland. Paul Gascoigne flicks the ball over Colin Hendry and volleys it in.

    Did you know? He celebrated lying on the ground while teammates squirted water into his mouth: the famous "dentist's chair".

    4Owen vs Argentina 1998

    1998 World Cup last 16 in Saint-Étienne against Argentina. Michael Owen, 18, runs from halfway, beats two defenders and scores.

    Did you know? That goal made him famous all over the world. Three years later he won the Ballon d'Or.
